# Lesson Proposal: Marketing Intelligence Foundation

Date: 2026-06-05
Brand: Bonhomia Suites

## Proposed Lessons

- Weekly marketing work should begin with Salud Financiera and occupancy gap analysis before creative production.
- The 50% occupancy target should be visible in briefs, media plans, and strategy explanations.
- Business travelers / companies are the first audience priority unless data or a time-sensitive local event strongly argues otherwise.
- Public experiences and known seasonal signals such as Luciernagas should influence campaigns before the season starts, not only while active.
- Production data can be used for marketing strategy only as aggregate metrics; artifacts must not contain PII or secrets.
- The old reservation video pipeline should remain archived as historical reference, not as the default campaign generator.

## Review Notes

Promote any lesson that remains true after the first few weekly strategy runs into `knowledge/playbook.md`.
