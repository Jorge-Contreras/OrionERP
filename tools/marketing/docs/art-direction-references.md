# Art Direction References

This file stores reference rules for Bonhomia creative quality. Use these as principles, not as layouts to copy.

## User Reference Images

The two user-provided Airbnb-style images are not brand references for Bonhomia. They are design-rule references only:

- Strong first-read hierarchy: one large idea, then supporting copy.
- A simple graphic device can carry the composition: circle crop, geometric rails, collage rhythm, or central lockup.
- Negative space is part of the design, not leftover space.
- Photos should be treated as intentional graphic crops, not small pasted tiles.
- The layout should feel designed before it feels decorated.

## External Reference Rules

- Codex may use available image-generation, editing, local composition, or screenshot tools, but no tool should be asked to solve final logo placement, final public text, or factual suite imagery without Codex review.
- Meta photo ads are mobile-first and benefit from simple, clear imagery with concise messaging and a recognizable focal point. Source: https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/photo-ad-format
- TikTok creative work should preserve the concept and hook even when video generation is unsupported in V1; future video should prioritize a fast hook, clear value, and direct CTA. Source: https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/
- Google's ABCD framework is useful for future video and animated media: attract attention, brand early, connect to the audience, and direct action. Source: https://www.thinkwithgoogle.com/marketing-strategies/video/youtube-abcds-effective-video-ads/
- Visual hierarchy should guide what the viewer reads first, second, and last. Source: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/visual-hierarchy-ux-definition/
- Gestalt principles support cleaner layouts through proximity, similarity, continuity, and figure-ground discipline. Source: https://www.interaction-design.org/literature/topics/gestalt-principles
- Swiss/International Typographic Style is a useful discipline for Bonhomia business ads: grid, asymmetry, restrained palette, strong typography, and functional clarity. Source: https://www.aboutswitzerland.eda.admin.ch/en/swiss-style-forever-the-story-of-a-graphic-design-tradition

## Bonhomia Application

- Business/direct-booking ads may be brand-led posters. Do not force a suite photo unless the asset names a suite or a factual room image truly supports the claim.
- Event/experience ads may use generated atmosphere as the hero, then a real suite module only when the copy connects the event to staying at Bonhomia.
- Destination/awareness ads may use the logo and graphic composition alone.
- Financial strategy can identify which suites need promotion, but visual quality still matters. If a campaign does not name a specific suite, choose an approved editorial suite photo or omit room imagery.
- Generated event or atmosphere layers must not show rooms, hotel exteriors, balconies, amenities, people, readable signage, or anything that could be mistaken for a Bonhomia property feature unless the source is a real approved Bonhomia asset.

## Quality Questions

Ask these before accepting a final image:

- Can the viewer understand the ad in two seconds on a phone?
- Is there one dominant focal point?
- Does the brand mark feel integrated into the layout?
- Does every photo have a reason to be there?
- Would the creative still look intentional if the background image were replaced?
- Could any generated object or scene mislead a guest about the suite or property?
