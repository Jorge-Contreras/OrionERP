# Pending Lessons - Bonhomia Reservation Video

Status: proposed

Promote these only if they remain useful after review:

- Phone-frame capture layouts made the Bonhomia website easier to distinguish than full-screen cropped screenshots.
- PayPal sandbox automation can fail at the popup or iframe boundary; campaign scripts should preserve the handoff and explain the payment step.
- The first voice pass was too slow and formal; Bonhomia social videos need relaxed Spanish, shorter lines, and faster pacing.
- Synthetic house music was useful as a technical placeholder but not publish-ready quality.
- Rendering and metadata checks should not depend only on system `ffmpeg` being on `PATH`; Remotion/Node fallbacks are useful.
- Capture scripts should write a manifest even after partial failure so later render/review steps can degrade gracefully.
