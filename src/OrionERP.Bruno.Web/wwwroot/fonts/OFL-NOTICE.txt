Fonts bundled with brunosgarden.com
===================================

Anton  — Copyright (c) Vernon Adams, Cyreal. SIL Open Font License 1.1.
Inter  — Copyright (c) The Inter Project Authors. SIL Open Font License 1.1.

Both families are used here under the SIL Open Font License, version 1.1,
which permits self-hosting and embedding in a website. Full licence text:
https://openfontlicense.org/

Files are the Latin and Latin-Extended woff2 subsets published by Google
Fonts; they are served from this folder so the site makes no third-party
font requests (the Content-Security-Policy in Program.cs only allows
first-party font sources).

To refresh them, download the woff2 files referenced by:
  https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Anton&display=swap
  https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Inter:wght@100..900&display=swap
and keep the unicode-range declarations in wwwroot/css/brunos-site.css in sync.
